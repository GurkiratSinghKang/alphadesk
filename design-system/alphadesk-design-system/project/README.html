The Design System Nov 2025
A design system for a systematic trading terminal — built for humans who'd rather read the research than refresh the chart. Editorial serif. Tabular mono. One accent: liquid gold.

§ 01 · Principles

Four rules the system never breaks.

Every token, component, and layout in this system follows four rules. If a design breaks one of them, it's off-brand — even if it "looks good." Taste is not a tie-breaker.

01

Quiet by default, loud by accent.

Ink and paper (warm near-blacks) carry the page. Liquid gold (#c9a66b) is used once per view, not three times. Never gradient. Never glow except for live status.

02

Serif for voice, mono for truth.

Editorial italic serif carries strategy names, memos, and hero copy. Tabular mono carries every number. UI sans sits between them. Never swap their jobs.

03

P&L is chartreuse and coral.

Not traffic-light red/green. Profit is a8d04d, loss is e07856. The goal is to look at losses without flinching — and to notice when profit gets too interesting.

04

Borders do the depth work.

Shadows are hairlines. Radii stay under 10px except for modals. Density is a feature; white space is earned, not sprinkled.

§ 02 · Foundations

One stylesheet, one source of truth.

colors_and_type.css defines every token: ink scale, gold stops, P&L semantics, the three font families, the type ramp, spacing, radii, and motion. Import it in every artifact. Do not invent colors; extend the scale.
